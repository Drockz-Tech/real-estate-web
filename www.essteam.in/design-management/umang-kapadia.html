    Umang Kapadia

    Sr. Interior Designer - Associate

    Since 2022

    Umang Kapadia studied Interior Design (B.ID) at Veer Narmad South Gujarat University and has fourteen years of professional experience in the field. He has been associated with Essteam since June 2022, contributing to projects such as Ajay Lakhwala Bungalow, M/s Stalwart Realties, Sudeep Shroff/Dr. Mona Shroff Apartment, M/s Velox Automation Pvt. Ltd. Factory, and Dholakia Farm Development Rahulbhai Dholakia Bungalow‑2.

    With a strong understanding of design development and project execution, Umang brings a practical and confident approach to his work. He focuses on understanding design requirements, resolving details, and ensuring effective project outcomes. His ability to combine creativity with precision allows him to contribute meaningfully to the team’s work, while continuing to refine his skills through diverse project experiences.
